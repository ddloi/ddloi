## 👨‍💻 About Me

> *"Turning raw data into structured insights & automated, scalable pipelines."*

Hey there! 👋 I'm **Đặng Đại Lợi** (`ddloi`), an undergraduate **Data Science student** at **HUFLIT** (Ho Chi Minh City University of Foreign Languages & Information Technology), deeply passionate about **Data Engineering**, scalable cloud architectures, and machine learning pipelines.

- 🎓 **Education:** Undergraduate in **Data Science** @ **HUFLIT** *(In Progress)*
- 🎯 **Career Goal:** Aspiring **Data Engineer**
- 🔭 **What I'm Learning & Engineering:**
  - Designing automated **ETL / ELT workflows** and robust data pipelines
  - Relational database schema architecture with **PostgreSQL**, **MySQL**, and **MSSQL**
  - Workflow orchestration and distributed scheduled pipelines using **Apache Airflow**
  - Cloud-native analytics, storage, and querying on **Google Cloud Platform (BigQuery)**
  - Containerizing development & production with **Docker** & automated CI/CD via **GitHub Actions**
- ⚡ **Fun Facts & Mindset:**
  - 💡 *“Data is the new oil”* ⛽ — but only truly valuable when refined and structured.
  - 🤖 If I have to do something more than twice, I automate it.
  - 🌱 Actively building real-world projects & open to **Data Engineering Internship** opportunities!
